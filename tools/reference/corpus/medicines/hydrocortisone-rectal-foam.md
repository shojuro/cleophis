# Hydrocortisone rectal foam

Hydrocortisone rectal foams have been discontinued by the manufacturer.

For information about other prescription medicines for ulcerative colitis and similar bowel conditions, speak to your doctor or a pharmacist.

To treat piles (haemorrhoids), hydrocortisone also comes as cream, ointment and suppositories. If you are looking for information on this, you can read about hydrocortisone for piles and itchy bottom.

## Related conditions

- Piles (haemorrhoids)
- Ulcerative colitis
