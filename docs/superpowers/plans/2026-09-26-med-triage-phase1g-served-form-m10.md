## What the survey found (2026-09-26, read-only; the reason this phase exists)

1. **One artefact.** The Q6_K base built on the M8 and M9 pods hashes identically on all six rung-builds: sha256 `2588912fe87f55b8381b9fc8faacd0e905c9eb2db641a468be693f45ad6fc87a`, 1,673,006,944 B (Q5_K_M likewise `39b4cc4d…`, 1,471,805,280 B). llama.cpp b10042 is pinned on both the pod and `tools/pipeline`. The served base can be pinned as bytes.
2. **The triage hero cannot be installed from the app today** (pre-existing, independent of quantisation):
   - `catalog::hero_variant` returns `base_model: None` for a flat entry, so `begin_tier_switch` hands the FE an empty base model and `downloadHeroPair("")` finds nothing.
   - The signed dist catalog (v10, live) carries NO triage adapter for `Qwen3-1.7B`; the only `kind: adapter` for that base is the tutor's behavioral adapter (`61ac4957…`), and selection is by `kind + base_model`, so a download would land the wrong adapter and `verify_adapter_once` would refuse the launch.
   - The bundled `modelFile`/`adapterFile` basenames (`Qwen3-1.7B-Q4_K_M.gguf`, `triage-armb-v3-Qwen3-1.7B.gguf`) do not match the dist basenames `download_artifact` writes (`<app_data>/models/<dist basename>`), so nothing would ever read as installed. The tutor tiers match; the triage entry does not.
   - The device harness sidesteps all three: it reads hand-placed files and re-hashes them against the bundled catalog — correct, but it means the "phone build" has never been the app's own download path.
3. **Selection must be by sha.** Publishing a second base (Q6_K) or a second adapter for the same `base_model` makes `kind + base_model` ambiguous. The entry already pins `sha256`/`adapterSha256`; the selector should use them.
4. **The pipeline hardcodes Q4 and one adapter name.** `publish.py` resolves every base under a local `Q4_K_M/` subdir; `build_catalog.py` derives adapter basenames as `behavioral-v1-<base>` only (the live catalog's `tutor-v1.3`/`voice-adult-v1` adapters were published by another route). A Q6_K base and a `triage-v3` adapter cannot pass through either script unchanged.
5. **Signing and publishing need the founder's secrets.** `CURATOR_KEY_FILE` and the B2 credentials live in `tools/pipeline/.env` outside the repo; neither `.env`, `.venv` nor the pinned `llama-quantize` is present on this machine. This phase prepares everything to "ready to sign and publish" with a runbook; the two secret-bearing commands are the founder's.
6. **The loader and the download policy do not block.** Launch verifies only sha256 (no size/RAM check at load); download preflight = catalog size + 200 MiB free; the metered prompt fires at ≥ 50 MB and the charge notice at ≥ 1 GB for Q4 and Q6_K alike; `mobile_supported` = RAM ≥ 4 GB, `n_ctx` 2048 on the low tier. Q6_K adds ≈ 0.4 GB to the file. The Rust test pinning the Q4 sha literal and the harness's Q4 tok/s estimate are ordinary edits.
7. **The gate side.** The frozen gate (`gate_on_pod.py` b91f0409) serves Q4; the sibling serves f16 + extras for the ENDPOINT suite only (`--only probe-triage-heldout`). Crisis, harm, embedded and every other bar have only ever been read at Q4. `artifact_identity` compares the run manifest's `base_q4_sha256` with the sibling's; it has no notion of a registered served form.
8. **A correction to the 2026-09-26 recommendation on the prompt clause.** M9's clause (iii) hits on v11a sit under pA 1 / pB 2 / pC 0 / pF 1; pA and pF do NOT carry the fourth disposition ("you cannot judge this one — say so…"), and pC does. The claim that the clause feeds clause (iii) is not supported by the per-condition data (n ≤ 2 per cell). A prompt variant remains cheap to serve, but it is exploratory: registered as REPORTED with no prediction, or dropped (founder's call, below).
