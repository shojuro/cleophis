# Phase 1h — the floor tier, part one: product control and the $0 measurements (APPROVED 2026-09-27)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:subagent-driven-development (parallel agents in worktrees, per-task review, whole-branch review per repo). Steps use checkbox syntax. Draft 1 was rewritten after an adversarial design review (6 blocking / 14 important / 9 minor findings, every one accepted; the rulings are at the end). Approved by the founder on 2026-09-27 as drafted.

**Goal:** Put the two things the floor tier owns under product control — the crisis line and the reference lookup — and take every measurement the floor-tier registration needs, for $0 and with no pod, so that Phase 1i can register M11 on anchors rather than guesses.

**Founder decisions this plan implements (2026-09-27):** the 1.7B is the FLOOR TIER (safety floors on the model; capability bought up the ladder); the crisis line is the product's (a fixed block REPLACES the reply on a plain disclosure; the model reply is shown only when it carries a red-flag route); cited doses are allowed from a reference pack, uncited never; grounded reference lookup is the fabrication lever; retrieval on the phone is LEXICAL ONLY (no embedder on Android); A33 licenses extractive, LLM-free training rows from the licensed reference corpus under the review's conditions; two rounds — this one has no pod.

**Architecture:**
- **Detector r3** closes the registered blind spot (crisis-embedded-12, "wanting to end things") with the narrowest pattern, under the R75 procedure, in both ports and both repos.
- **The product crisis rule** is implemented in the guard and MEASURED FIRST on M10's served replies through the post-guard tools, with the route set that keeps the reply read in two variants ({EMERGENCY} and {EMERGENCY, CLINICIAN}); the numbers, not the design, decide what Phase 1i registers.
- **The reference lookup** = a curated, curator-signed NHS pack bundled in the triage build (Open Government Licence), a lexical retriever (FTS5 + a title index with a deterministic "did you mean"), a triage-specific prompt contract, a per-turn LOOKUP mode that runs the crisis detector before anything else, shows citations, withholds uncited doses and overdose-section citations, and is single-turn. NO_EVIDENCE is a scripted refusal; the model never sees it.
- **The reference probe family** runs through its OWN runner (never in the default suite), assembles byte-for-byte the product's prompt (one shared assembler, golden-tested in Rust, JS and Python), and reads four arms: real entity, invented entity, crisis-in-lookup, symptoms-in-lookup. Its $0 baseline is read on the untrained rungs locally (CPU llama.cpp at the pinned tag).
- **The A33 rows** are cut from a disjoint slice of the pack and validated, but NOT trained here.

**Spec:** triage `docs/spec.md` R73–R82 (+ R83 new: the floor tier, detector r3, the product crisis rule, the reference family); MVP spec §11 A24 (amended 40 of 41), A31, A32 (+ A33 new). Phase 1g merged (triage main d8e00a0; mobile/triage-p2 eeda6a0).
